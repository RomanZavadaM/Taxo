Taxo / Driver Worktime
Version: 8.70 candidate r11
Candidate date: 2026-09-17
Published baseline after merge: 8.70 candidate r10
Development baseline: verified v8.70 candidate r10 checkpoint

Candidate r11 adds a standalone 60-calendar-day driver activity register. The PDF contains a daily summary and exact minute intervals for driving, other work, availability, breaks, rest, absence and undefined time. Every day is controlled to 24:00. Confirmed/manual tachograph intervals have highest priority; automatic recognition is clearly marked as a candidate; table/worklog and confirmation-form intervals are used as additional sources; calculated break/rest periods are explicitly labelled, and missing information remains «Невизначено» instead of being silently treated as rest.

Candidate r10 adds single-driver work-time analysis improvements: the №340 protocol window can generate and immediately open its current PDF, and it shows an hourly weekly balance with separate work-time (60:00) and driving-time (56:00) limits. The 4-month 48:00 average remains a separate control.

v8.70 starts the waybill issuance workflow from the graphical driver schedule.
Waybill r5: simple two-column route paste, internal automatic day rollover, and calendar dates on every printed overnight/multiday waybill field. All preserved v8.66 and v8.70 development is consolidated into main.
Candidate r9: configurable local, network and cloud-synchronized workspaces contain every mutable business file. A shared lock enforces sequential multi-copy use, safe migration preserves the previous workspace, and database file references are portable between computers.
Waybill archive: issued PDFs are registered in the persistent database, receive a unique official series+number and an internal yearly id, and are stored under the selected workspace in Output/Waybills/YYYY/MM.
Route model from v8.66 r10 remains: one route = one exact time scenario.
Tachograph recognition algorithm is unchanged.

Distribution: routine r11 verification uses START.bat through taxo_app.py. The automatic source-candidate workflow produces a versioned START archive after merge. Windows/macOS executable builds remain reserved for selected checkpoints. No user database is bundled.
