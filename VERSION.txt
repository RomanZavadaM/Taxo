v8.70 starts the waybill issuance workflow from the graphical driver schedule.
Waybill r4: simple two-column Excel/text route paste, automatic D+ rollover and endpoints, one numbered waybill with a full date interval for overnight trips, and safe Driver-role completion without deleting history.
Waybill archive: issued PDFs are registered in the persistent database, receive a unique official series+number and an internal yearly id, and are stored under Documents/DriverWorktime/Output/Waybills/YYYY/MM.
Route model from v8.66 r10 remains: one route = one exact time scenario.
Tachograph recognition algorithm is unchanged.

Distribution: START.bat source candidate plus GitHub Actions Windows Setup/Portable and native macOS arm64/x86_64 candidate builds. No user database is bundled.
